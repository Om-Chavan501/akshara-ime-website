Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

What we collect

To activate and manage your license, we store: your email address, a one-way hashed identifier of each Mac you activate (not any other information about your device), and your purchase/license history. We do not collect what you type — AksharaIME's text conversion happens entirely on your own Mac and is never sent anywhere.

Who processes your data

  • Paddle.com — payment processing (Merchant of Record). See Paddle's privacy policy.
  • Supabase — account authentication and license storage.
  • Resend — delivering sign-in emails.

Sign-in

We use passwordless email sign-in (a one-time code sent to your email) — we never store a password.

Your rights

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing us.
