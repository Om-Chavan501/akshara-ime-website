How it works

Three steps, and you're typing Marathi everywhere on your Mac.

  1. Install AksharaIME

    A normal macOS installer. It adds AksharaIME as a selectable input source in System Settings — nothing else changes.

  2. Switch to it from the input menu

    Pick AksharaIME from the menu bar or with your usual input-switch shortcut, same as switching to any other keyboard layout.

  3. Type phonetically, anywhere

    TextEdit, Notes, Word, your browser, Slack — type mahArAShTra and get महाराष्ट्र, right in place, as you type.
